# Nx

The mature, industry-standard [monorepo](/blog/monorepo) tool, with a large plugin ecosystem. Python support exists via community plugins but can feel bolted on compared to its native JavaScript/TypeScript tooling, and it relies on the local machine already having the right language versions installed rather than managing them itself.

A strong option if ecosystem size and plugin maturity matter more than first-class polyglot support — compared here against [Moon](/blog/moon) and [Turborepo](/blog/turborepo).

Gut feeling so far: not a fan. Nothing specific broken about it, just does not click the way Moon does for a polyglot setup.
